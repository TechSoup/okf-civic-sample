# What I Need Funding For

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.** Illustrative only; not part of the published
civic/0.6 collection. See the [collection README](../../README.md).

We stock what our neighbours eat, which means our grocery bill doesn't move
the way a typical food-shelf budget moves — a Hmong grandmother's shelf and a
Somali family's shelf are not the same shelf, and neither one is the
prepackaged assortment a regional food bank allocation assumes. Culturally
appropriate procurement costs more per household, not less, and it's the
first thing a generic grant formula misunderstands about us.

We are seeking funding to expand our home-delivery route for homebound
elders, who are the households most likely to fall out of contact entirely
if we can't reach them; to keep garden plots available to more families who
want to grow their own food rather than only receive it; and for general
operating support that lets us keep buying what our communities actually
cook with, instead of what's easiest to source. We are a 4.5-FTE
organization built almost entirely around one relationship-holding executive
director — flexible support that doesn't require us to build out grant
-specific reporting infrastructure matters more to us than a larger,
narrower award would.
