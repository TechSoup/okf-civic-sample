# Volunteer constraints & preferences

> **⚠ Synthetic.** In a real bundle **this file is the organization's to edit.** Note the frontmatter: `generated.by` is `human:org-staff`, not an agent. That is the actor convention (OKF v0.2 §7) recording who owns this document — and it is how a tool reading the bundle can tell that this page is not its to rewrite.

*Pre-seeded with defaults for a very small immigrant-serving food organization, then revised by the organization — mostly to say no to things it has been offered before.*

## We are four and a half people

Everything else on this page follows from that. We have **4.5 FTE and sixty volunteers**, and every hour that goes into a project comes out of the shelf being open. That is not a figure of speech — it is the actual trade.

So: **we need small, finished things.** A project that takes three weeks and is done is worth more to us than a project that takes three months and is better. If you can only offer the second one, we would rather you offered it to a bigger organization.

## Please do not redesign our website

We have been offered this twice and we are going to be direct about it.

Yes, the site looks like 2018. Yes, the photographs are old. **That is not our problem.** Our problem is that the donation form on it is running software nobody has updated in three years, taking about $70,000 a year from people who trust us, and **we are not sure anyone here has the password to the admin panel.**

A volunteer who fixes that and changes nothing else visually has done exactly what we need. A volunteer who arrives with a design concept has heard our situation and substituted a more interesting one.

If the fastest safe path happens to involve a new site, fine — but the goal is a donation channel we can trust and administer, not a better-looking organization.

## About the volunteer who built it

The person who built our site in 2018 was generous and did us a favour and we are grateful. Then they moved away.

**We would like to not be here again.** Whatever you build or move us onto, the test is: *can our executive director, who is not technical, keep this running and get back into it if she needs to?* If the answer requires you, it is the wrong answer. Managed hosting we pay for beats something clever we host. Boring beats elegant.

We would also like the **domain registration and every credential in the organization's own name**, on an organizational payment method, written down somewhere our board chair can find. We think our domain might be renewing on a card belonging to somebody who left.

## Money and donor data

- **The donation channel is money in trust.** Anything you touch there needs to be safer when you finish than when you started, and if you are uncertain, stop and tell us rather than proceeding carefully.
- **Donor records are properly kept and we would like them to stay that way.** Our donor database is reconciled against our books monthly and it works. Do not migrate it, restructure it, or connect anything to it without a specific reason we have agreed to.
- **Do not build anything that connects shelf data to donor data.** They are separate on purpose.

## Our shoppers

- **We do not record names or immigration status at the shelf, ever.** Households, household size, neighbourhood. That is it, and it is firm. Some of the people who shop here have very good reasons not to be on a list.
- **Nothing on the website may collect information about people who use the shelf.** No "register for a visit," no intake form, no email capture on the shelf-information page. Donors and shoppers are different audiences and the site should keep them that way.
- **Photographs of shoppers require explicit permission**, per person, per use, and if there is any doubt we do not use it. If you touch the site, do not carry forward any image you cannot show us a permission for.

## Language

Our shelf serves households speaking **Hmong, Karen, Somali, Oromo, and Spanish** before English. If you are working on the website, know that **the site is not how we reach them** — that happens in person, through volunteers from those communities, and on our Facebook page. The website is for donors and partners.

That means: do not spend your limited time adding a translate widget to the site. It would not reach the people you are imagining, and we have seen that advice before.

## Working with us

- **Our executive director is the contact and she is also everything else.** She has genuinely about two hours a week for this. Come with specific questions and a short list.
- **Remote is fine.** We do not need you here, though you are welcome on a shelf day if you want to understand us.
- **We can pay for hosting.** We are small, not broke. If the right answer costs $30 a month, say so — we would rather pay than accept something fragile.
- **Confidentiality agreement** for anything touching donor records. Not needed for website work that stays away from them.
- **Handover in writing, in plain language**, including how to get back in. That is the deliverable we care most about.

## What we would say no to

- A website redesign.
- Migrating our donor database.
- Anything connecting shelf data to anything.
- A solution that requires you, or anyone with your skills, to keep working.
