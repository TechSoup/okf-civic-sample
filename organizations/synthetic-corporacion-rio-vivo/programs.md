# Programs & services

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

*The list below is **derived** (simulated) from public materials; a real organization would confirm and replace it with its own description. In a real Colombian bundle the Spanish would be canonical.*

- **Community water monitoring** — about thirty points on two tributaries, sampled by trained community monitors using field instruments, with periodic accredited-laboratory verification. The technical core, and the source of the safety considerations in [README](README.md).
- **Technical support in *consulta previa*** — helping communities understand and respond to what is proposed for their territory. The organization supports; the community consults. It does not speak for anyone.
- **Administrative and legal action** — *acciones populares*, *tutelas*, and interventions in environmental licensing. Brought with or on behalf of communities, on their instruction.
- **Accompaniment of the eleven community organizations** — the connective work: showing up, translating between a technical process and an *asamblea*, and being available when something happens at three in the morning. Hardest to describe and most of what the organization does.
- **Environmental education** — with schools and community groups, in Spanish, oral and practical.
- **Coalition practice** — methodology exchange with [Riverbend Air](../synthetic-riverbend-air-alliance/README.md) in Detroit and [Gulf Corridor](../synthetic-gulf-corridor-justice-project/README.md) in Louisiana.

## Accompaniment is the programme and it is nearly unreportable

*Acompañamiento* is the organization's central activity and it does not produce countable units. It is presence, availability, and relationships that took a decade. There is no output measure that captures it, and every attempt produces something misleading — meetings attended, kilometres driven, calls answered.

Worth noting because it is a recurring problem for organizations like this: **the work that matters most is the work a reporting framework cannot see**, so it gets described as overhead or as a vague preamble to the countable programmes. Funders fund the monitoring; the monitoring works because of the accompaniment.

## Which of these should become its own file first

**Community water monitoring**, but not for the usual reason. It should be separated because it is the programme with **safety implications, a data-sovereignty arrangement, and eleven separate owners** — and because folding it in with environmental education means those considerations are not stated anywhere near the activity that generates them.

**Consulta previa support** is the second candidate: a distinct legal process with its own timelines, its own risk of being co-opted, and a clear boundary about who speaks that deserves stating explicitly rather than implicitly.

## A note the coalition makes visible

The three coalition organizations run **recognizably the same programme** — community-led environmental monitoring feeding regulatory intervention. Placed side by side:

| Who owns the data | The organization | The organization | **The eleven communities** |
| Principal risk | Losing the data | Data authenticity challenged | **Someone being killed** |

**Same method, three risk environments, three incompatible sets of correct practice.** A coalition sharing a "common data format" — which these three do — has to accommodate that, and a format designed around the first two would push this organization toward publishing things it must not publish.

That is a real design lesson for anyone building shared schemas across an international network, and it is why this coalition triangle is in the collection.
