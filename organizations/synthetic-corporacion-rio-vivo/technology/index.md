# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Río Vivo runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup-network / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Read this inventory with one thing held firmly in view: some of the data described here could get someone killed.** Not as a figure of speech. Monitoring point coordinates, sampling schedules, and community monitor names, combined, describe where specific named people in a contested rural area can be found at predictable times. Colombia is among the most dangerous countries in the world to be an environmental defender. See [README](../../../README.md).

That inverts the ordinary reading of a technology inventory. **Several absences here are protections. Several conveniences are hazards.** A recommendation to consolidate, publish, map, or integrate — advice that is correct almost everywhere else in this collection — may be actively dangerous here.

**And note that the most important dataset is not the organization's.** Community monitoring data belongs to eleven community organizations, held under agreements that constrain what Río Vivo may do with it. The bundle schema has no way to say that.

Technology gaps here feed [technical-volunteers](../technical-volunteers/index.md).
