# What I Need Funding For

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.** Illustrative only; not part of the published
civic/0.6 collection. See the [collection README](../../README.md).

The river is not a resource here. It is where people bathe, fish, wash, and
bury. When a company obtains a licence to discharge into it, somebody has
decided that those things matter less than the licence, and we exist to make
the argument that they don't — alongside eleven community organizations who
own the data our monitoring produces, not us.

We are seeking funding for our accredited-laboratory verification checks,
which our field-instrument monitoring depends on to hold up under technical
challenge; for the digital-security practices that keep a location dataset
from becoming a targeting package, since community leaders here take real
personal risk; and for general operating support that doesn't require us to
hand over underlying data our communities haven't agreed to share. A funder
asking for that data may be told no, and we need that to be an acceptable
answer.
