# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what the Law Center runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Read this inventory for the absences.** Most bundles in this collection are interesting for what an organization has. This one is interesting for what it has deliberately refused: no web analytics, no third-party trackers, no marketing pixels, no client-facing chat. Each of those absences is a decision made to protect people whose contact with an institution can have immigration consequences, and each of them reads to an automated maturity assessment as a deficiency.

That inversion — **sophistication that scans as immaturity** — is the reason this bundle is in the collection.

Technology gaps here feed [technical-volunteers](../technical-volunteers/index.md).
