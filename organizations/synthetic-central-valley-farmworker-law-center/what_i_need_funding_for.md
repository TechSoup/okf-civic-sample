# What I Need Funding For

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.** Illustrative only; not part of the published
civic/0.6 collection. See the [collection README](../../README.md).

The Law Center represents farmworkers in Fresno and adjacent counties in wage
claims, housing-condition cases, workplace-safety and pesticide-exposure
matters, and a small removal-defense practice. Most of what reaches us starts
the same way: an advocate on a Valle Verde pantry route, taking an intake at
the tailgate, because people tell us what's happening to them at a food line
long before they'd ever walk into a law office. Our know-your-rights sessions
in community halls, churches, and labor camps do the same work in reverse —
they're outreach, not representation, but they're where most of our eventual
cases begin.

We are seeking funding to expand tailgate intake to more pantry routes and
labor camps, sustain our removal-defense capacity (currently a fraction of
requests, rationed by attorney availability rather than need), and build
better ways to stay in contact with clients whose cases outlast their time in
the county — a wage claim can run eighteen months; a seasonal worker may only
be here for five. Flexible general support also lets us respond quickly to
heat-emergency and mass-layoff spikes in demand, and to sudden shifts in
immigration enforcement, without waiting on a grant cycle built for slower
problems.
