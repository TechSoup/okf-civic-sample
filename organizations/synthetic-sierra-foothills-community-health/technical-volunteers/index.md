# Technical volunteers

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

A bundle with two kinds of thing:

- [constraints](constraints.md) — **the org's own** rules and preferences for volunteers. In this bundle they are largely **not preferences**: they are HIPAA obligations, and the organization has no authority to waive them.
- **Requests** — one file per project, each a draft the org owns and can post:
  - [ehr-consolidation](ehr-consolidation.md) — finish a clinical migration that stopped in 2023, starting with finding out how many patients actually exist twice.

**Method:** to turn this bundle into a request, an agent reads the org's [README](../../../README.md), [technology](../technology/index.md) (inventory + capability), and [constraints](constraints.md), then frames a project grounded in the org's actual gaps — and marks it a draft the org edits and blesses before anything goes out.

## The threshold question, before anything else

**This is the only project in the collection that cannot legally begin without a signed Business Associate Agreement.** A volunteer whose work brings them into contact with protected health information is a business associate under HIPAA, and that is a contractual and regulatory relationship, not a goodwill arrangement. No BAA, no project — and the organization will not be talked into a workaround, because the exposure is theirs.

That single fact rules out most casual volunteering here and shapes everything about how the request below is scoped: **as much of the work as possible is designed to happen without the volunteer ever seeing PHI**, precisely so that the parts requiring the BAA are as small and as supervised as they can be.
