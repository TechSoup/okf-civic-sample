**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Sierra Foothills runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Two things to hold while reading the inventory.**

**Clinical systems are a different category.** This organization's electronic health records, practice management, and dental system are procured, contracted, regulated, and supported in a world that has nothing to do with the donated-and-discounted software market. They are not in the TechSoup lane and never will be. A bundle that only sees the TechSoup lane sees the least important half of this organization's technology.

**Everything here touches PHI or sits one hop away from it.** That changes what a "volunteer project" even means — see [constraints](../technical-volunteers/constraints.md), where the requirement is a legal instrument rather than a preference.
