# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Riverbend Air runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Read this bundle's inventory differently from the others.** For most organizations in this collection, technology supports the program. Here the technology *is* the program: 31 sensors, a data pipeline, and a public map are what this organization does. That inverts the usual reading — a gap in the inventory is not an efficiency problem, it's a **program integrity** problem, and a silent sensor failure is a missed advocacy opportunity rather than an inconvenience.

Technology gaps here feed [technical-volunteers](../technical-volunteers/index.md), and in this bundle that link is unusually direct.
