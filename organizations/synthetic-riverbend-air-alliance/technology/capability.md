## Why this org's profile is shaped oddly

Most small nonprofits in this collection are weak on infrastructure and weak on data. Riverbend Air is the inverse of the usual pattern: **strong technical skill, weak technical stewardship.** The staff can site a sensor, calibrate a gateway, and read a time series. Nobody is minding whether the data will still exist in five years, whether the residential addresses are adequately protected, or whether a sensor that stopped reporting last Tuesday has been noticed.

That distinction is invisible to a single overall maturity score, and flattening these six domains into one number would produce a figure that describes this organization badly. Something to consider before anyone builds that average.

*When real assessment data arrives it replaces this table and the warning comes off. The weak domains here — data stewardship and privacy, not skills — are the raw material for [volunteer projects](../technical-volunteers/index.md).*
