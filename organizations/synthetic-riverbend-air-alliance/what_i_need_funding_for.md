# What I Need Funding For

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.** Illustrative only; not part of the published
civic/0.6 collection. See the [collection README](../../README.md).

Thirty-one sensors on thirty-one neighbors' houses, reading every two
minutes, and the data belongs to the block it was collected on. That network
is our whole program, which means it's also our whole vulnerability: our
sensors are a well-understood commodity type with real limitations, and
nobody has ever independently calibrated ours against a reference monitor.

We are seeking funding for an independent calibration study against a
reference-grade monitor, so our numbers can withstand the challenge they'll
eventually get at a permit hearing; to replace aging sensors before drift
becomes unreliability; and for general operating support that keeps our
youth science corps running, since the residents who understand the data
best are the ones we trained ourselves. A funder who wants durable evidence,
not just more sensors, is the funder we need most right now.
