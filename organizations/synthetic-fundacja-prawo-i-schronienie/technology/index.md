# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Prawo i Schronienie runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup-network / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Two things about this inventory are unlike anything in the twelve US bundles.**

**The catalogue is not the same catalogue.** Donated and discounted software reaches Polish NGOs through the **TechSoup Global Network partner serving Poland**, not through TechSoup US, and the available offers differ by country because the donating companies decide market by market. Most consequentially for a small NGO: **QuickBooks is not available — it is a US-only product.** So the organization's accounting software was simply bought, at commercial cost, like any business would. See the inventory for what that changes.

**GDPR turns some procurement decisions into legal decisions.** Whether a service is hosted in the EEA, and what happens to personal data if it is not, is a compliance question with a regulator attached — not a preference. That is the substance of the [volunteer project](../technical-volunteers/index.md), and it is the reason a US-shaped technology assessment would miss this organization's most significant exposure entirely.
