# Programs & services

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

*The list below is **derived** (simulated) from public materials; a real organization would confirm and replace it with its own description.*

Motor City Trades runs one pathway with several entrances and one exit that matters.

- **Pre-apprenticeship training, three tracks** — electrical, HVAC, and welding. 14–18 weeks, classroom plus shop hours, ending in the entrance exam for a registered apprenticeship.
- **Math bridge** — 6 weeks, for the ~40% who place below the arithmetic level the entrance exams require. Not remedial framing; the organization treats it as the first course rather than a prerequisite.
- **Apprenticeship and job placement** — relationships with two union halls and eleven employers, including the negotiation work of getting a returning citizen an interview in a trade with licensing complications.
- **Second-chance track** — the reentry-contracted cohort, about a third of enrollment. Same training, different intake, different wraparound, and considerably more employer negotiation.
- **Wraparound supports** — transportation stipends, tool purchase, work boots, childcare vouchers, and a small emergency fund. Unglamorous and, by the organization's own account, the largest single driver of completion.
- **Three-year follow-up** — the organization's signature claim and its weakest data. Staff contact graduates annually to establish whether they're still in the trade. It is done by phone, email, and sometimes social media, tracked in a spreadsheet by one person. See [README](README.md).

*(Job training is the activity behind its [J22](../../_shared/ntee/J22.md) classification; the placement operation is why it also carries [J20](../../_shared/ntee/J20.md).)*

## Which of these should become its own file first

The **second-chance track**, without much doubt. It has a separate funder (a county contract), separate reporting, a distinct intake process, its own eligibility rules, and a set of employer relationships that exist only for it. It is currently described as a variant of the main program and it is really a program with its own everything.

The **three-year follow-up** is the second candidate, for the opposite reason: it barely exists as a program at all, and giving it its own file would make its thinness visible instead of letting it sit as one bullet next to five solid ones.

## A note on counting

Enrollment, completion, and placement are reported to three different funders on three different cycles with three different definitions of "placed." The organization can produce any of those numbers on request and cannot easily produce a single reconciled version, which is the substance of the [volunteer project](technical-volunteers/index.md). If you are building anything that aggregates program counts across this collection, this bundle is the one that will make you define your terms.
