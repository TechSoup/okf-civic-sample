# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Black Mountain runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Two things make this inventory unusual.**

**The organization's technology is partly a public utility.** It runs workstations that graduates use to do their remote jobs, and a laptop lending library that has drifted well past its original purpose. Both exist because the market and the public sector did not provide something, and both mean this organization's technology serves people who are not its staff. Most inventory models assume technology supports an organization's own operations.

**Its reporting burden is out of proportion to its size.** Twelve staff, a federal subaward, a state allocation, and a transition grant — three reporting regimes with three definitions of a placement, and no data person. That is the [volunteer project](../technical-volunteers/index.md).

Technology gaps here feed [technical-volunteers](../technical-volunteers/index.md).
