# Programs & services

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

*The list below is **derived** (simulated) from public materials; a real organization would confirm and replace it with its own description.*

- **Community sampling** — air and water sampling on a schedule and in response to incidents, using methods chosen for defensibility rather than convenience. Some samples go to an accredited laboratory, which is expensive and is why there are fewer of them than the organization would like.
- **Health survey** — door-to-door, administered by trained community members, covering respiratory symptoms, household cancer history, pregnancy outcomes, and residence duration. Several thousand households. The organization's most distinctive evidence and its most contestable. See [population](population.md).
- **Regulatory intervention** — technical comments, hearing testimony, and formal objections in permitting proceedings. This is where the sampling and the survey are aimed.
- **Public document archive** — permits, agency monitoring reports, correspondence obtained through records requests, and the organization's own sampling results, published and searchable. **The community's evidence base**, and the subject of the [volunteer project](technical-volunteers/index.md).
- **Community organizing** — five staff working block by block: turnout for hearings, training residents to testify, and the slow work of making a permitting proceeding legible to people who have thirty days to respond to four hundred pages.
- **Legal referral** — the organization is not a law firm. It refers residents to counsel and supports litigation with technical work.
- **Green-jobs pathway** — with [synthetic-Crescent City Career Lab](../synthetic-crescent-city-career-lab/README.md): remediation and coastal-infrastructure work is coming to the corridor, and the organization's position is that people who live with the pollution should get the jobs cleaning it up. Gulf Corridor knows which work is real and which employers are credible; Career Lab trains and places.

*(Pollution monitoring and abatement advocacy is the activity behind its [C20](../../_shared/ntee/C20.md) classification; the conservation framing of the corridor and coastal work is why it also carries [C30](../../_shared/ntee/C30.md).)*

## The archive is a program, and it should have its own file

**This is the clearest "should become a folder" case in the collection.**

The document archive is currently one bullet. It is:

- The **community's evidence base** — the only place where twenty years of permits, monitoring reports, and records-request returns are gathered and searchable.
- Used by residents, journalists, academic researchers, and opposing counsel.
- **Cited in proceedings**, which means its integrity is a legal matter and not a website matter.
- A **target**: defaced once, scraped continuously, and hit by traffic events that coincide with hearing dates. See [inventory](technology/inventory.md).
- Growing, with no documented retention, provenance, or chain-of-custody practice.

Describing it as a program bullet understates it by a wide margin. It is infrastructure that a region's environmental litigation partly depends on, run by a sixteen-person nonprofit.
