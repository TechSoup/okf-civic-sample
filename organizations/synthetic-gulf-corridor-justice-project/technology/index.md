# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Gulf Corridor runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**The priority order here is inverted from every other bundle in this collection.**

Nearly all security thinking is organized around confidentiality — keep the secrets in. This organization's most important data is **meant to be public**. Its problems are:

1. **Integrity** — can it prove a document in its archive is authentic and unaltered? Because if an opposing expert can raise doubt about that, they do not need to rebut the contents.
2. **Availability** — is the site up on the day of a hearing? Traffic events have coincided with hearing dates more than once.
3. **Confidentiality** — a distant third, and confined to one thing: the health survey's respondent-level data.

A security recommendation set built around protecting secrets will miss what this organization needs. Read the inventory with that reordering in mind.
