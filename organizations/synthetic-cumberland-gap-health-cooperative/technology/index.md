# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Cumberland Gap Health runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**One thing to hold while reading: the connectivity problem here belongs to the patients.** The clinic's own internet is mediocre and workable. Its patients' internet is the binding constraint, and it is not something the organization can buy, configure, or train its way past. Nearly all digital-inclusion advice is written for the opposite situation — an under-connected organization serving a connected public — and applying it here produces recommendations the organization has already tried.

The inventory also carries the collection's clearest example of **a document pile as a technology problem**. This is a fourteen-person clinic that receives most of its inbound clinical information as unstructured faxes, and someone sorts them by hand.

Technology gaps here feed [technical-volunteers](../technical-volunteers/index.md).
