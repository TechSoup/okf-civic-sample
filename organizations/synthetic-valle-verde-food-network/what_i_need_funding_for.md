it is deciding between groceries and the water bill, and the community they

missing, to expand bottled-water distribution as more wells fail nitrate and

promotoras — from the communities they serve, speaking Spanish, Mixtec, and
Triqui — are the reason anyone trusts a truck showing up. We do not collect
names or addresses at distribution and will not add that requirement for a
funder's headcount; general operating support that respects that boundary is
