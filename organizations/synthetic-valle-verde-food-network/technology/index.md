# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Valle Verde runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Read the inventory with one fact in front of you: the network is not there.** Several of the 17 communities on Valle Verde's routes have no reliable cell coverage, and a driver-coordinator can be out of contact for most of a working day. Nearly every default assumption in nonprofit technology advice — cloud-first, real-time, mobile app, sync on save — quietly assumes a network. Here it isn't a constraint to design around, it's the ground condition.

The second thing to hold: **WhatsApp is load-bearing infrastructure** at this organization, and it arrived without anyone deciding.
