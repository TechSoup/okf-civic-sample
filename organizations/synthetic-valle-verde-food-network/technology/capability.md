## Why "Staff digital skills" cannot be scored here

The rubric asks about staff digital skills as though the staff were one population using one set of tools. This organization has two workforces:

- **Office staff** — 8 people, fully inside Microsoft 365, comfortable with the accounting and reporting platforms. Genuinely Established.
- **Route coordinators and promotoras** — 15 people whose working tools are a clipboard, a vehicle, a phone, and WhatsApp. Assessing them as "developing" against a rubric built around desktop productivity software would produce a number that means nothing. They are highly skilled at the actual work, using the tools that work in the conditions they work in.

Scoring the second group low would suggest the fix is training them on Microsoft 365, which would help nobody. **The gap is not in the people, it's in the absence of tools designed for a vehicle, a dead zone, and a two-week cycle.**

This is a real limitation of any single-axis maturity rubric applied to an organization with a field workforce, and it is why this bundle carries a split rather than a score.

## The domain that actually matters

**Reporting & measurement, At risk** — and specifically the two-week lag. Everything else on this list is tolerable. The lag means the organization cannot respond to a demand spike until it has already happened, in a service population whose need swings by a factor of two seasonally. That is the finding, and it is the [volunteer project](../technical-volunteers/index.md).
