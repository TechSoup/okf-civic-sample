# Technical volunteers

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data. And note that this bundle's [verification determination has expired](../verification.md).**

A bundle with two kinds of thing:

- [constraints](constraints.md) — **the org's own** rules and preferences for volunteers. The org edits this; an agent scoping a project must honor it.
- **Requests** — one file per project, each a draft the org owns and can post:
  - [job-posting-analysis](job-posting-analysis.md) — find out what regional employers are actually asking for, from the postings themselves, and keep the curriculum pointed at it.

**Method:** to turn this bundle into a request, an agent reads the org's [README](../../../README.md), [technology](../technology/index.md) (inventory + capability), and [constraints](constraints.md), then frames a project grounded in the org's actual gaps — and marks it a draft the org edits and blesses before anything goes out.

## Why this project is in the collection

It is the **only AI project of the three here that touches no personal data at all.** The other two — [the Law Center's](../../synthetic-central-valley-farmworker-law-center/technical-volunteers/intake-triage-assist.md) intake work and [the clinic's](../../synthetic-cumberland-gap-health-cooperative/technical-volunteers/inbound-document-routing.md) document routing — both required careful containment because a model would come near people's information. This one analyzes **public job postings**, which are somebody's marketing material.

That makes it a useful contrast case: **the same technology, in the same collection, with the risk profile of a spreadsheet.** If your framework for evaluating AI proposals treats all of them as similarly sensitive, this bundle is where that shows.

The interesting risk here is different and worth noticing: not privacy, but **the temptation to let a model's reading of the labor market drive what people are trained for.** [constraints](constraints.md) addresses it.
