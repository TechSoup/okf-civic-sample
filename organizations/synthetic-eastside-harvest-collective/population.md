# Who Eastside Harvest serves

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

Eastside Harvest serves **households on Detroit's east side**, concentrated in the roughly two square miles around its four growing sites. Its distribution is **open** — no income documentation, no residency proof, no referral required — which is a deliberate program choice and also the reason its data on who it serves is thinner than a means-tested program's would be.

Scale: about **4,200 households a year** receive produce, and the paid **youth farm crew** runs 18–24 young people per season, ages 16–24. *(**derived**, simulated — reconstructed from distribution counts, not from a participant registry.)*

The open-door design has a consequence worth stating plainly: the organization **counts households served, not people served**, and it does not collect demographics. Anyone asking this bundle "how many children did you reach" will not find an answer, because the organization decided not to ask. That is a defensible choice and an inconvenient one, and both facts belong here.
