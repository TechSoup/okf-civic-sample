# Technology & capacity

**⚠ Synthetic — fabricated data.**

The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what North Star runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Read this inventory with the threat model in mind.** Several things that look like weaknesses here are decisions: short retention, minimal logging, no analytics, work kept off cloud services the organization can't control. Several things that look fine are the actual problems. Distinguishing them requires knowing what the organization is defending against, which is written out in [constraints](../technical-volunteers/constraints.md).

**And read it as a growth story.** The organization roughly quadrupled between 2017 and 2021. Its systems are not so much contradictory as **disconnected** — each one works, and the seams between them are where 1,300 matters and 90 outside attorneys fail to line up.
