The concept the **digital-assessment team owns** — what Nyando Health runs, how we know, and its capacity to take on more. A folder because it holds parts with different owners and lifecycles:

- [inventory](inventory.md) — the technology the organization has, each item tagged with **how we know** (derived / acquired-via-TechSoup-network / sourced-directly).
- [capability](capability.md) — where the org sits on the [assessment.techsoup.org](https://assessment.techsoup.org/) rubric. *(Mocked — to show the slot.)*

**Read this inventory expecting to be impressed, and then notice what it costs the promoters.**

This is the **best field data operation in the collection.** 142 community health promoters capturing structured household data on mobile devices, offline, syncing when they reach connectivity, feeding a national health information system monthly, with supervision that catches errors before they compound. Four other organizations in this collection have the same connectivity problem — [Fresno](../../synthetic-valle-verde-food-network/technology/inventory.md), [Letcher County](../../synthetic-cumberland-gap-health-cooperative/technology/inventory.md), [the Sierra Foothills mobile unit](../../synthetic-sierra-foothills-community-health/technology/inventory.md), and [the Cauca basin](../../synthetic-corporacion-rio-vivo/technology/inventory.md) — and all four use paper. **This one solved it.**

Two things sit underneath that achievement and both belong in the reading:

**The promoters partly pay for it.** Mobile data and airtime for the organization's required reporting come substantially out of stipends. That is a transfer from the poorest people in the operation to the organization's donor deliverables.

**The data goes up and does not come back.** Everything the promoters collect flows into a national system the organization cannot easily query for its own management. That is the [volunteer project](../technical-volunteers/index.md).
