# SDG 1 — No poverty

**A shared classification node.** Organizations carry this goal number in optional `x-civic.sdg`; the Members list below is generated from that key by `scripts/build_hubs.py`.

**End poverty in all its forms everywhere.**

## Why only two members

Almost every organization in this collection addresses poverty. Two carry the code.

That gap is worth noticing. A food shelf, a legal-aid practice defending someone from wage theft, a rural clinic seeing uninsured patients, a workforce programme moving people out of low-wage work — all of them are anti-poverty work by any reasonable reading, and eleven of the thirteen do not claim the goal.

**SDG codes here reflect self-description**, and organizations tend to claim the goal that names their *activity* rather than the goal that names their *purpose*. A food organization claims [SDG-02](SDG-02.md) because it is about food. It is also about poverty and does not say so.

The consequence for anyone querying: **an SDG-based rollup of anti-poverty work in this collection will find two organizations and miss most of it.** Not a fault in the sample data — a property of what happens when a broad goal competes with a specific one for the same organization's self-classification.
