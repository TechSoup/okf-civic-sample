## Five members, and this is the collection's immigrant-serving hub in practice

Four of the five work primarily with immigrant, refugee, or displaced communities. The fifth is a rural health centre serving a majority-public-insurance patient population.

Notice what has happened: **this broad goal has become, in practice, the international-and-immigrant hub**, because it is the goal that reaches [the Warsaw foundation](../../organizations/synthetic-fundacja-prawo-i-schronienie/README.md) when [P84](../ntee/P84.md) cannot.

**Neither hub is the right answer to "find the immigrant-serving organizations."** The precise one is incomplete; the complete one is imprecise. That is the whole trade between the two vocabularies, in one comparison, and it is the reason both layers exist in this collection.

## The goal's second half is doing no work here

"Reduce inequality **within and among** countries." Every member of this hub is addressing inequality *within* a country. **Nothing in this collection addresses inequality among countries**, and nothing could — these are all locally-operating service and advocacy organizations.

Worth noting because the goal's text invites a reading the membership does not support, the same hazard [SDG-04](SDG-04.md) has with "education."
