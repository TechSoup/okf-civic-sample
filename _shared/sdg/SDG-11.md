# SDG 11 — Sustainable cities and communities

**A shared classification node.** Organizations carry this goal number in optional `x-civic.sdg`; the Members list below is generated from that key by `scripts/build_hubs.py`.

**Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable.**

## Three urban members, and the rural absence is the finding

All three members are in cities — Detroit twice, New Orleans once. **No rural organization in this collection carries this goal**, and six of the fifteen are rural or serve rural populations.

The goal's text says "**cities and human settlements**." A hollow in Letcher County is a human settlement. An unincorporated community in Fresno County with no grocery store and failing wells is a human settlement whose sustainability is precisely at issue.

None of them claim it, and the reason is almost certainly the word *cities* sitting first in the goal's short name. **The label is doing more work than the definition**, and the effect is systematic: a rollup of "sustainable communities" work in this collection returns three urban organizations and misses the places where settlement sustainability is most acutely in question.

This is a small, concrete instance of something worth watching for across any SDG-classified corpus. **Goals get claimed on the strength of their short titles**, and short titles carry connotations the definitions do not.

## What the three members actually share

Less than the hub suggests. One grows food on vacant urban land; two monitor industrial pollution in fenceline neighbourhoods. What they have in common is that all three are **working on the physical conditions of a specific set of city blocks** — which is a real shared quality, and closer to what this hub means here than "sustainability" in any general sense.
