# SDG 4 — Quality education

**A shared classification node.** Organizations carry this goal number in optional `x-civic.sdg`; the Members list below is generated from that key by `scripts/build_hubs.py`.

**Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.**

## Three members, all workforce organizations, and none of them a school

All three organizations here train adults for jobs. None is an educational institution, none awards a degree, and their "education" is 10 to 20 weeks aimed at a credential an employer will accept.

The goal's own language covers this — **lifelong learning** is in the text — but it is worth noting that a query for education organizations in this collection returns three vocational programmes and nothing else. There is no school, no tutoring programme, and no youth education organization among the fifteen.

**A reader who assumed "SDG 4" meant schooling would misread all three.** That is a general hazard with broad goals: the label invites an assumption the membership does not support, and nothing in the code corrects it.

*Note that the exemplar this collection was modeled on — the real Chapter 510 bundle — is a youth writing and publishing centre that carries SDG 4 for something much closer to what the goal usually evokes. Its absence here changes the character of this hub entirely.*

## The three report their outcomes incompatibly

Same three organizations as [J22](../ntee/J22.md), and the comparison there is the important one: **one publishes a long-term retention figure resting on a single spreadsheet, one declines to publish any long-term figure at all, and one reports partial wage-gain data with honest caveats.** The most careful presents worst.

If you are ranking organizations on education or training outcomes, read [J22](../ntee/J22.md) before you trust any of these numbers.
