**Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts.**

## The coalition hub — three organizations, three countries, one method

This is the only hub in the collection whose membership is **exactly the `coalition_with` triangle**: the three fenceline-monitoring organizations, in Detroit, New Orleans, and Cali, Colombia.

That makes it the best place to see the collection's cross-geography structure, because here the **classification edge and the relationship edge agree** — these three are both similarly classified and actually connected. Elsewhere in this collection that alignment is rare; most co-members of a hub have no relationship at all.

**And it is the hub that demonstrates why a shared standard across a network is hard.** All three run recognizably the same programme, share a data format, and require **incompatible practices**:

| | Detroit | New Orleans | Cali |
|---|---|---|---|
| Publishes monitoring locations | Yes | Yes | **No — a safety decision** |
| Names contributors | Hosts confidential | — | **Never** |
| Owns the data | The organization | The organization | **Eleven communities** |
| Principal risk | Losing the record | Authenticity challenged | **Someone being killed** |

A data format designed around the first two would push the third toward publishing what it must not publish. See [C20](../ntee/C20.md), where the same table appears alongside the note that **NTEE can only reach two of these three**.

## None of them would describe this as climate work first

Worth being honest about. All three carry SDG 13, and all three would say their work is about **what people are breathing and drinking now**. The climate framing is real — industrial emissions, watershed degradation, and coastal loss are climate matters — and it is also the framing that funders reward, and the organizations know it.

Their own primary self-descriptions run through health ([SDG-03](SDG-03.md)), justice ([SDG-16](SDG-16.md)), and water ([SDG-06](SDG-06.md)). **Climate is the frame that travels best, which is not the same as the frame that fits best.**
