## What this code means

Outpatient facilities, known also as free clinics and community health centers, that have been established by the community and which provide basic medical care including physical examinations, immunizations, family planning, nutrition assistance and diagnosis and treatment of common ailments. Services are often provided on an ability-to-pay basis when they target low-income and indigent residents of the community. Include mobile health clinics.
