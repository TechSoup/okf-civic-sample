## What this code means

Promotion of a clean environment by establishing minimum water quality standards; developing and enforcing regulations to reduce water pollution; testing, recording and reporting on the quality of water in lakes, rivers and streams; issuing warnings and investigating hazards related to contamination; and, when warranted, initiating litigation against individuals and organizations that have failed to comply with water pollution control regulations. For activities addressing general water conservation, general water pollution or specific bodies of water (such as Chesapeake Bay), use Water resources. For promotion or provision of clean drinking water, use Clean water supply (in Public health).
