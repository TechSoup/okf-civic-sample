## What this code means

Preservation and protection of water resources from indiscriminate waste and activities to ensure that the supply of quality water is adequate to meet the needs of society, agriculture and industry. Also included are projects to preserve and manage coastal lands including shorelines, coastal waters and lands extending inland from the shore which affect coastal waters; bays, lakes, rivers, wetlands, estuaries, watersheds and other aquatic habitats. See also: Clean water supply (in Public health).
