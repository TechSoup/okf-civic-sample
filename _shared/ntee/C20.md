## What this code means

**Pollution abatement and control services** — work aimed at reducing or preventing contamination of air, water, or land. In this collection it covers community monitoring paired with regulatory intervention, which is the shared method of all three environmental-justice organizations here.

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** — see [index](index.md).*

## The most important hub in this collection for understanding context

Three organizations in this collection run **recognizably the same programme**: community members collect environmental data, the organization analyses it, and the result is used to intervene in a permitting or enforcement process. They are tied together by a `coalition_with` edge that ignores geography, and they share a data format.

Two of them are here. The third is in Colombia and cannot carry an NTEE code.
