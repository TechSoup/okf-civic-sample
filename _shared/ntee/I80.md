# I80 — Legal services

## What this code means

**Legal services** — direct legal representation and advice, typically for people who could not otherwise obtain it. Distinct from [I83](I83.md) (public interest law), which describes impact and systemic litigation rather than individual representation.

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** — which matters unusually much for this hub; see below.*
