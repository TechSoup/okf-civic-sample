## What this code means

**Ambulatory health centers and community clinics** — outpatient primary care delivered by a community-based organization, typically on a sliding scale and typically to people who would otherwise go without. Distinct from [E30](E30.md) (health treatment facilities, outpatient), which one member carries in addition.

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** — see [index](index.md).*

**The statute does not scale with revenue.** Both organizations must conduct a HIPAA risk assessment, protect PHI to the same standard, and meet the same breach-notification duties. One has an apparatus for it. The other has a practice manager who also sorts the fax pile.

That is not a finding about either organization. It is a finding about **how regulatory burden lands on small rural providers**, and any analysis that scores these two side by side on cybersecurity without saying so is producing a comparison that misleads.
