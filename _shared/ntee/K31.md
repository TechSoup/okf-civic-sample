# K31 — Food banks, food pantries

**A shared classification node.** Organizations carry this code in optional `x-civic.ntee`; the Members list below is generated from that key by `scripts/build_hubs.py`. NTEE is a US-only layer, so it reaches twelve of the fifteen.

## What this code means

**Food banks and food pantries** — the warehousing, sourcing, and distribution-point infrastructure of food assistance, as distinct from the act of getting food to people that [K30](K30.md) describes. In practice most organizations doing one do the other, which is why both members here carry both codes.

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** — see [index](index.md).*

## The distinction between K30 and K31 is doing less work than it looks

Both members of this hub also carry [K30](K30.md), and neither organization would describe itself as being in two categories. One runs a warehouse and nine truck routes; the other runs a shop-style shelf with a cold room. **Both are, unavoidably, doing food banking and food distribution.**

Worth noting as a general property of classification systems rather than a criticism of this one: **codes drawn to distinguish activities often distinguish functions within a single organization**, and the result is that a small nonprofit accumulates codes without becoming more legible. A reader seeing "K30, K31" learns that this is a food organization, which they already knew from either code alone.
