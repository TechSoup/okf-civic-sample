## What this code means

**Health treatment facilities, primarily outpatient** — the facility-based framing of outpatient care, as distinct from the community-clinic framing captured by [E32](E32.md). In practice the two overlap almost entirely, and the single member here carries both.

## Why only the larger clinic carries this

Both community health organizations in the US bundles deliver outpatient care. Only [Sierra Foothills](../../organizations/synthetic-sierra-foothills-community-health/README.md) carries E30 alongside [E32](E32.md), and the reason is scale rather than activity type: it operates **three fixed sites, owns two buildings, and runs dental and behavioural health service lines**, which reads as a facility operator in a way that a fourteen-person clinic with one site does not.

So this code is functioning as a **proxy for size**, not for a different kind of work. Both organizations do outpatient treatment in facilities.

That is worth flagging because it recurs throughout this collection: **several signals that look like they describe what an organization does actually describe how big it is.** The [technology maturity rubric](../../organizations/synthetic-frogtown-community-table/technology/capability.md) does the same thing, scoring infrastructure Established for organizations that can afford contracted IT support. A corpus with a twentyfold budget range will have size leaking into a lot of fields that were not meant to carry it.
