# K30 — Food service, free food distribution

**A shared classification node.** Organizations carry this code in optional `x-civic.ntee`; the description lives here once, and the Members list below is generated from that key by `scripts/build_hubs.py`. NTEE is a US-only layer, so it reaches twelve of the fifteen.

## What this code means

**Food service and free food distribution programs** — organizations whose activity is getting food to people who need it, without charge. It describes *what an organization does*, distinct from the food-banking and warehousing infrastructure captured by [K31](K31.md) and from the growing operations captured by [K20](K20.md).

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing before relying on the exact string. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** and does not apply to the three international organizations in this collection — see [index](index.md).*

## Why this hub is worth looking at

**Three members, in three states, doing recognizably different work under one code.** This is the largest hub in the collection alongside [P84](P84.md) and [J22](J22.md), and it demonstrates both the value and the limit of a shared classification node.

The value: a query for food-security organizations finds all three.

The limit: **one runs mobile pantry routes to communities with no grocery store, one farms vacant urban land, and one stocks a shop-style shelf for five immigrant communities' cooking.** Their procurement, storage, staffing, language requirements, and definitions of success have almost nothing in common. K30 is true of all three and tells you very little about any of them.

Worth holding when a classification code is used as a proxy for similarity.
