# J22 — Job training, vocational training

## What this code means

**Job training and vocational training** — programmes that teach occupational skills, typically leading to a credential or a placement. Distinct from [J20](J20.md) (employment procurement assistance), which describes the placement work itself.

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing — this part of the J range has several near-neighbours with similar names, and the exact title should be verified rather than trusted from this file. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** — see [index](index.md).*
