# C30 — Natural resources conservation and protection

**A shared classification node.** Organizations carry this code in optional `x-civic.ntee`; the Members list below is generated from that key by `scripts/build_hubs.py`. NTEE is a US-only layer, so it reaches twelve of the fifteen.

## What this code means

**Natural resources conservation and protection** — protecting air, water, land, and ecosystems. Distinct from [C20](C20.md) (pollution abatement and control), which describes acting against a specific contamination source.

*Title given as commonly published; confirm against the current NTEE-CC listing. **NTEE is a US IRS vocabulary** — see [index](index.md).*

## Both members are here for a framing reason rather than a programme reason

Both organizations in this hub carry [C20](C20.md) as their primary code and C30 in addition, and neither one runs a conservation programme in the sense a land trust would recognize. No acquisition, no easements, no habitat restoration.

They carry it because **their work is described in conservation terms as well as in pollution terms**, and both framings are true: an organization contesting an air permit in an industrial corridor is abating pollution and protecting a shared resource, depending on which sentence you write.

Worth noting as a property of secondary `x-civic.ntee` codes generally: **they often record how an organization has learned to describe itself to funders**, rather than a distinct activity. In a corpus assembled from self-reported classifications, that is most of what secondary codes are — which does not make them useless, but does mean they measure discourse as much as work.

An honest reading of both these organizations is that they are **health organizations that work through environmental regulation**, which is neither code and is closer to what their staff would say.
